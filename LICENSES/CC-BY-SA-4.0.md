This licence applies to the ISO 10628-2 symbol data and the material it derives from:

- `js/iso-symbols.js` (symbol geometry, connection points, translated names)
- `tools/iso-symbols/sheets/*.svg` (source sheets, unmodified)
- `tools/iso-symbols/tr.py` (symbol names and translations)

Original work: "ISO 10628-2:2012 Symbols" sheets 1–7 (identifier BK-2020-04-PID),
by Steven Baltakatei Sandoval, 2020, published on Wikimedia Commons (https://w.wiki/dg9),
project repository https://gitlab.com/baltakatei/BK-2020-04/.

Changes made: symbols were extracted automatically from the sheets, cut into one entry per symbol,
given connection ports from the "preferred connection locations" marked on the sheets,
re-scaled, given normalised stroke widths, and given French and Dutch names.
A few typos in the English names were corrected.
